How to interpret this comparison · sources & privacy

Observed BGP neighbours

RIPE RIS collects BGP paths from participating route collectors. Left and right describe an ASN’s position in those paths. They are not provider/customer labels. “Uncertain” observations may result from a collector’s own peering and are excluded by default. Address-family filtering requires positive IPv4 or IPv6 observation counts; absence of an observation is not proof that a relationship does not exist.

Listed exchanges and facilities

PeeringDB is maintained by its participants. Exchange matches use exchange IDs, collapsing multiple LAN records. The address-family filter uses listed exchange addresses; it does not test connectivity. Facility matches use facility IDs and are independent of the family filter. A listed membership does not guarantee active peering or a physical path. No PeeringDB record means no directory evidence, not an absent network.

Dates, saved pairs and requests

“Observed” is the RIPE data timestamp; “checked” is when the source was fetched. PeeringDB listing update dates may be much older. Refresh failures keep any previous evidence and display its original date. Exported and saved pairs preserve the evidence you inspected. Notes and comparison names stay in the browser; saving locally is explicit. New ASN lookups send each ASN separately to RIPEstat and PeeringDB. These services also receive ordinary request metadata such as your IP address. No account or API key is required for visitor lookups.
